# What's new

## 0.7 (19 November 2026), still a prototype

- **What you'll be able to do**: three outcomes at the top, so you know what the activity is for.
- **Before you go**: a pause at the end with one question to think about (nothing to type, nothing saved), ideas if you're stuck, and a look back at the three outcomes.
- **A lighter map**: 37 KB instead of 198 KB, with no visible change at the page's sizes. It loads when you scroll near it, and if it can't load, the place list and table carry the same information.

## 0.6 (19 November 2026), still a prototype

- **Titled a prototype** everywhere: the page title, a "Prototype" label above the name, link previews, the citation, the README and the GitHub description. It stays until the design is set.
- **A clear start**: one "Start with a case" button in the opening, and what's in the record so far (evenings saved, cases checked, weather stations, latest evening). No winner in the opening, so the guess in step 02 stays a real guess.
- **Charts**: average miss by how far ahead (a dot plot, the gap drawn between the two models) and evening by evening (two lines, with a readout that follows the pointer or the arrow keys). Each has a spoken summary and a table with the same numbers.
- **Chart colors** checked for color blindness and contrast in both themes; circles and squares tell the models apart as well as color.

## 0.5 (19 November 2026)

- **Rain on the page**: under each case ("And rain?"), as a table in the bigger picture (caught, missed, false alarms, rightly dry), and as a choice in the scenario.
- **Rain is called carefully**: no model is called better at rain until the record holds at least 10 rainy place-evenings, because one rainy evening counts up to three times. For now it says "too early to call".
- **Rain follows the units switch**: inches with °F, millimeters with °C.
- **Icons for finding your way**: line icons beside every entry in "On this page" and beside each section's number, and a Rain entry in the page list.

## 0.4 (19 November 2026)

- **Public**, after a protocol check. Live at https://davidpetry-cloud.github.io/two-forecasts-one-sky/
- **About and methods**: the data, how cases are compared, how a winner is called, rain, limits, updates, accessibility, privacy, sources, and how to cite.
- **"Too close to call"**: a model is only called better when its advantage is bigger than the day-to-day ups and downs; every count shows its share.
- **Open data**: download every case as CSV or JSON (CC0).
- **Rain is being saved** each night (both models and the stations), ready for the page.
- **Feedback**: report a problem or suggest an idea on GitHub.
- US spelling; "Back to top" at the end of every section; link previews and a tab icon.
- The nightly archive heals itself after outages, and the footer says how fresh the data is.
